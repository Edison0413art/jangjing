02 / Who we are

The quiet metals that keep industry running.

Long before lithium made the headlines, Zimbabwe's pegmatites and the Great Dyke were yielding the minor metals that modern industry cannot function without. Jangjing Private Limited works that proven ground — mining and trading the specialty metals behind alloys, electronics, ceramics and defence.

We hold our own claims and sign exclusive purchase agreements with small-scale and artisanal miners across the Mutoko, Hurungwe, Kadoma and Great Dyke regions — turning scattered, informal output into consistent, documented, export-grade supply. In collaboration with the University of Zimbabwe, we ground our geology and metallurgy in solid technical work.
